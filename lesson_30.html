🔑 Key Ideas You Must Know

Konsep penting yang wajib dikuasai
1. Gradient (m)
m = y₂ − y₁x₂ − x₁
How steep the line is: change in y ÷ change in x.Kemiringan garis: perubahan y ÷ perubahan x.
2. Gradient–intercept form
y = mx + c
m = gradient, c = where the line crosses the y-axis.m = gradien, c = titik potong dengan sumbu-y.
3. Point–gradient form
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
Use it when you know the gradient and one point.Dipakai jika diketahui gradien dan satu titik.
4. Parallel lines
m₁ = m₂
Parallel lines have the same gradient.Garis sejajar memiliki gradien yang sama.
5. Perpendicular lines
m₁ × m₂ = −1  ⇔  m₂ = −1m₁
Flip the fraction and change the sign.Balik pecahannya dan ubah tandanya.
6. Intersection point
Solve the two equations simultaneously
Where two lines meet, both equations are true.Titik potong = penyelesaian SPLDV kedua persamaan.
⚠ Before finding a gradient: always rearrange the equation into y = mx + c first. For example, −3y = 2x + 5 has gradient −{2|3}, not 2.Selalu ubah persamaan ke bentuk y = mx + c dulu sebelum membaca gradiennya.

Question 1 – Find the equations of the lines

Tentukan persamaan garis-garis berikut
[2]1(a)A line with gradient −{1|2} that crosses the y-axis at y = 3.Garis dengan gradien −½ dan memotong sumbu-y di y = 3.
Gradient m = −{1|2}. The line crosses the y-axis at 3, so c = 3.Gradien m = −½ dan titik potong sumbu-y memberi c = 3.
Substitute into y = mx + c.Substitusi ke y = mx + c.
y = −{1|2}x + 3  (or x + 2y − 6 = 0)
[3]1(b)A line that passes through the points (2, −5) and (0, 8).Garis yang melalui titik (2, −5) dan (0, 8).
Find the gradient:
m = 8 − (−5)0 − 2 = 13−2 = −{13|2}Hitung gradien dengan rumus (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).
The point (0, 8) is on the y-axis (x = 0), so c = 8.Titik (0, 8) berada di sumbu-y, jadi c = 8 langsung.
Check with (2, −5): −{13|2}(2) + 8 = −13 + 8 = −5 ✓Cek dengan titik yang lain.
y = −{13|2}x + 8  (or 2y = −13x + 16)
Common mistake: mixing up the order, e.g. (8 − (−5)) / (2 − 0). Take the top and the bottom in the same order.Urutan pengurangan di pembilang dan penyebut harus sama.
[3]1(c)A line which has gradient 5 and passes through the point (4, −1).Garis dengan gradien 5 yang melalui titik (4, −1).
Use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) with m = 5, (x₁, y₁) = (4, −1):
y − (−1) = 5(x − 4)Gunakan rumus y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
Expand: y + 1 = 5x − 20Jabarkan kurungnya.
Subtract 1 from both sides: y = 5x − 21Kurangi kedua ruas dengan 1.
y = 5x − 21  Check: 5(4) − 21 = −1 ✓
[3]1(d)A line that is perpendicular to −3y = 2x + 5 and passes through (5, 2).Garis yang tegak lurus −3y = 2x + 5 dan melalui titik (5, 2).
Rearrange into y = mx + c by dividing everything by −3:
y = −{2|3}x − {5|3}  so  m₁ = −{2|3}Bagi semua suku dengan −3 agar terlihat gradiennya.
Perpendicular gradient: flip and change the sign:
m₂ = −1−2/3 = {3|2}  Check: −{2|3} × {3|2} = −1 ✓Gradien tegak lurus: dibalik dan diubah tandanya.
Use the point (5, 2): y − 2 = {3|2}(x − 5) → y − 2 = {3|2}x − {15|2}Substitusi titik (5, 2) ke rumus y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
Add 2: y = {3|2}x − {15|2} + {4|2} = {3|2}x − {11|2}Tambahkan 2 (= 4/2) ke kedua ruas.
y = {3|2}x − {11|2}  (or 2y = 3x − 11)
Common mistake: reading the gradient as 2 straight from −3y = 2x + 5. You must divide by −3 first.Kesalahan umum: langsung menganggap gradiennya 2.
−3y = 2x + 5answer
[3]1(e)A line that is parallel to y = −3x − 2 and passes through the point (−6, −3).Garis yang sejajar y = −3x − 2 dan melalui titik (−6, −3).
Parallel → same gradient: m = −3Sejajar → gradien sama.
y − (−3) = −3(x − (−6)) → y + 3 = −3(x + 6)Hati-hati dengan tanda negatif ganda.
Expand: y + 3 = −3x − 18 → y = −3x − 21Jabarkan lalu kurangi 3.
y = −3x − 21  Check: −3(−6) − 21 = 18 − 21 = −3 ✓
y = −3x − 2answer

Question 2 – Points on a line

Titik-titik pada sebuah garis
The line y = {3|2}x − 4 passes through the points P and Q. The point P has coordinates (a, 11). The point Q has coordinates (−2, b).Garis y = 3/2 x − 4 melalui P(a, 11) dan Q(−2, b).
Key idea: if a point is on a line, its coordinates make the equation true, so substitute them in.Titik yang terletak pada garis memenuhi persamaan garis itu, jadi substitusikan koordinatnya.
[2]2(a)Find a.Tentukan nilai a.
Substitute x = a, y = 11: 11 = {3|2}a − 4Substitusi x = a dan y = 11.
Add 4: 15 = {3|2}a → multiply by {2|3}: a = 15 × {2|3} = 10Tambah 4, lalu kalikan dengan 2/3.
a = 10  → P(10, 11)
[2]2(b)Find b.Tentukan nilai b.
Substitute x = −2: b = {3|2}(−2) − 4Substitusi x = −2.
b = −3 − 4Hitung: (3/2) × (−2) = −3.
b = −7  → Q(−2, −7)

Question 3 – Triangle from three lines

Segitiga yang dibentuk oleh tiga garis
⚠ Note about the question paper. As printed, AB: 3x − 3y = 5 and AC: x − y = −1 both have gradient 1: y = x − {5|3} and y = x + 1. They are parallel, so they never meet and there is no vertex A. This must be a typo. The worked solution below uses AB: 3x − 2y = 5, which gives a right-angled triangle as the question intends. Catatan: pada soal tercetak, AB dan AC sejajar (gradien sama-sama 1) sehingga tidak bisa membentuk segitiga. Pembahasan memakai AB: 3x − 2y = 5.
3x − 3y = 52x + 3y = 19x − y = −1
[6]3(a)Find the vertices of the triangle ABC whose sides are given by the lines:
AB: 3x − 2y = 5    BC: 2x + 3y = 19    AC: x − y = −1 Tentukan titik-titik sudut segitiga ABC.
Strategy: each vertex lies on two sides. A is on AB and AC, B is on AB and BC, C is on BC and AC. Solve each pair simultaneously.Setiap titik sudut adalah perpotongan dua sisi, jadi selesaikan setiap pasangan persamaan.
Vertex A = AB ∩ AC. From AC: x = y − 1.
Substitute into AB: 3(y − 1) − 2y = 5 → y − 3 = 5 → y = 8, x = 7.Metode substitusi.
A = (7, 8). Check in AB: 21 − 16 = 5 ✓; in AC: 7 − 8 = −1 ✓
Vertex B = AB ∩ BC. Eliminate y:
(3x − 2y = 5) × 3 → 9x − 6y = 15
(2x + 3y = 19) × 2 → 4x + 6y = 38
Add: 13x = 53 → x = {53|13}Metode eliminasi: samakan koefisien y lalu jumlahkan.
Then y = 3x − 52 = 159/13 − 65/132 = {94|26} = {47|13}.
B = ({53|13}, {47|13}) ≈ (4.08, 3.62)
Vertex C = BC ∩ AC. From AC: x = y − 1.
Substitute into BC: 2(y − 1) + 3y = 19 → 5y = 21 → y = {21|5}, x = {16|5}.
C = ({16|5}, {21|5}) = (3.2, 4.2)
A(7, 8),  B({53|13}, {47|13}) ≈ (4.08, 3.62),  C(3.2, 4.2)
[3]3(b)Show that the triangle is a right-angled triangle.Tunjukkan bahwa segitiga tersebut siku-siku.
Write each side as y = mx + c:
AB: y = {3|2}x − {5|2} → mAB = {3|2}
BC: y = −{2|3}x + {19|3} → mBC = −{2|3}
AC: y = x + 1 → mAC = 1Cari gradien ketiga sisi.
Test the pairs: mAB × mBC = {3|2} × (−{2|3}) = −1Hasil kali gradien = −1 berarti kedua garis tegak lurus.
So AB ⊥ BC, and the angle at B is 90°. Triangle ABC is right-angled at B. ∎
Alternative (Pythagoras): AB² = {4693|169}, BC² = {4693|4225}, AC² = 3.8² + 3.8² = 28.88, and AB² + BC² = 28.88 = AC² ✓. The gradient method is much quicker.Cara lain dengan Pythagoras, tetapi metode gradien jauh lebih cepat.
