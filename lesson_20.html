a. Spiral Review Warm-up (00:00 - 00:10) Tinjauan Spiral Pemanasan

Explanation Pembahasan

Today we will start by reviewing how to solve linear simultaneous equations using the substitution method. Hari ini kita akan mulai dengan meninjau cara menyelesaikan persamaan linear simultan menggunakan metode substitusi.

Substitution Method: Express one variable in terms of the other, then substitute it into the second equation. Metode Substitusi: Nyatakan satu variabel dalam variabel lainnya, lalu substitusikan ke persamaan kedua.

Example: Solve $y = 2x + 1$ and $x + y = 7$. Contoh: Selesaikan $y = 2x + 1$ dan $x + y = 7$.

Substitute $y$ into the second equation: $x + (2x + 1) = 7$. Substitusikan $y$ ke persamaan kedua: $x + (2x + 1) = 7$.

$3x + 1 = 7 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2$. $3x + 1 = 7 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2$.

Substitute $x=2$ back: $y = 2(2) + 1 = 5$. Solution: $x=2, y=5$. Substitusikan $x=2$ kembali: $y = 2(2) + 1 = 5$. Solusi: $x=2, y=5$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. $y = 3x - 2$ and $y = x + 4$
$3x - 2 = x + 4 \implies 2x = 6 \implies x = 3$. Then $y = 3(3) - 2 = 7$.
Solution: $x=3, y=7$.
2. $y = x - 1$ and $2x + y = 5$
$2x + (x - 1) = 5 \implies 3x = 6 \implies x = 2$. Then $y = 2 - 1 = 1$.
Solution: $x=2, y=1$.
3. $y = 4 - x$ and $x - y = 1$
$x - (4 - x) = 1 \implies 2x - 4 = 1 \implies 2x = 5 \implies x = 2.5$. Then $y = 4 - 2.5 = 1.5$.
Solution: $x=2.5, y=1.5$.
4. $y = 5x$ and $x + y = 12$
$x + 5x = 12 \implies 6x = 12 \implies x = 2$. Then $y = 5(2) = 10$.
Solution: $x=2, y=10$.
5. $y = 2x + 3$ and $3x - y = 2$
$3x - (2x + 3) = 2 \implies x - 3 = 2 \implies x = 5$. Then $y = 2(5) + 3 = 13$.
Solution: $x=5, y=13$.

b. Introduction & Real-World Connection (00:10 - 00:15) Pengantar & Koneksi Dunia Nyata

Explanation Pembahasan

Imagine a straight road (line) crossing a curved railway track (parabola). The point where they cross is the intersection. Bayangkan sebuah jalan lurus (garis) yang memotong jalur kereta berbentuk lengkung (parabola). Titik di mana mereka berpotongan adalah titik potong.

Algebraically, we set $y_{line} = y_{curve}$. Secara aljabar, kita samakan $y_{garis} = y_{kurva}$.

Example: A drone flies in a straight line $y = 2x$. A ball thrown upwards follows $y = -x^2 + 4x$. Find intersection. Contoh: Sebuah drone terbang dalam garis lurus $y = 2x$. Bola yang dilempar ke atas mengikuti $y = -x^2 + 4x$. Cari titik potongnya.

$2x = -x^2 + 4x \implies x^2 - 2x = 0 \implies x(x - 2) = 0$. $2x = -x^2 + 4x \implies x^2 - 2x = 0 \implies x(x - 2) = 0$.

$x = 0$ or $x = 2$. If $x=0, y=0$. If $x=2, y=4$. Points: $(0,0)$ and $(2,4)$. $x = 0$ atau $x = 2$. Jika $x=0, y=0$. Jika $x=2, y=4$. Titik: $(0,0)$ dan $(2,4)$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. $y = x + 2$ and $y = x^2$
$x + 2 = x^2 \implies x^2 - x - 2 = 0 \implies (x-2)(x+1) = 0$. $x=2$ or $x=-1$. Points: $(2,4), (-1,1)$.
2. $y = 2x - 3$ and $y = x^2 - 3x$
$2x - 3 = x^2 - 3x \implies x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$. Use quadratic formula: $x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 12}}{2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{13}}{2}$.
3. $y = -x + 4$ and $y = x^2 - 4x + 4$
$-x + 4 = x^2 - 4x + 4 \implies x^2 - 3x = 0 \implies x(x-3)=0$. Points: $(0,4), (3,1)$.
4. $y = x - 1$ and $y = x^2 - 5x + 5$
$x - 1 = x^2 - 5x + 5 \implies x^2 - 6x + 6 = 0$. $x = \frac{6 \pm \sqrt{36 - 24}}{2} = 3 \pm \sqrt{3}$.
5. $y = 3x$ and $y = x^2 - 2x$
$3x = x^2 - 2x \implies x^2 - 5x = 0 \implies x(x-5)=0$. Points: $(0,0), (5,15)$.

c. Review of Prior Knowledge (00:15 - 00:20) Tinjauan Pengetahuan Awal

Explanation Pembahasan

To solve quadratics, we need to factorise and expand brackets. Untuk menyelesaikan kuadrat, kita perlu memfaktorkan dan menyederhanakan kurung.

Factorising: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. Find numbers that multiply to 6 and add to -5: -2 and -3. Memfaktorkan: $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. Cari angka yang dikali 6 dan dijumlahkan -5: -2 dan -3.

$(x-2)(x-3) = 0 \implies x=2$ or $x=3$. $(x-2)(x-3) = 0 \implies x=2$ atau $x=3$.

Expanding: $(2y - 2)^2 = (2y)^2 - 2(2y)(2) + (-2)^2 = 4y^2 - 8y + 4$. Menyederhanakan: $(2y - 2)^2 = (2y)^2 - 2(2y)(2) + (-2)^2 = 4y^2 - 8y + 4$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. Factorise $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$
Numbers that add to -7 and multiply to 12: -3 and -4.
$(x-3)(x-4) = 0$.
2. Expand $(3y + 1)^2$
$(3y)^2 + 2(3y)(1) + 1^2 = 9y^2 + 6y + 1$.
3. Factorise $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$
$2x^2 - 4x - x + 2 = 0 \implies 2x(x-2) - 1(x-2) = 0 \implies (2x-1)(x-2) = 0$.
4. Expand $(x - 4)(x + 4)$
Difference of squares: $x^2 - 4^2 = x^2 - 16$.
5. Solve $x^2 - 9 = 0$
$x^2 = 9 \implies x = \pm 3$.

d. Problem Introduction (00:20 - 00:25) Pengenalan Masalah

Explanation Pembahasan

Now we combine linear and quadratic equations. Given $y = 9 - x$ (line) and $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$ (curve). Sekarang kita menggabungkan persamaan linear dan kuadrat. Diketahui $y = 9 - x$ (garis) dan $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$ (kurva).

Set them equal: $9 - x = x^2 - 2x + 3$. Samakan keduanya: $9 - x = x^2 - 2x + 3$.

Rearrange to $ax^2+bx+c=0$: $x^2 - x - 6 = 0$. Susun ulang ke $ax^2+bx+c=0$: $x^2 - x - 6 = 0$.

Factorise: $(x-3)(x+2) = 0$. So $x=3$ or $x=-2$. Faktorkan: $(x-3)(x+2) = 0$. Jadi $x=3$ atau $x=-2$.

Find $y$: If $x=3, y=6$. If $x=-2, y=11$. Points: $(3,6)$ and $(-2,11)$. Cari $y$: Jika $x=3, y=6$. Jika $x=-2, y=11$. Titik: $(3,6)$ dan $(-2,11)$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. $y = 5 - x$ and $y = x^2 - 2x + 3$
$5 - x = x^2 - 2x + 3 \implies x^2 - x - 2 = 0 \implies (x-2)(x+1)=0$. Points: $(2,3), (-1,6)$.
2. $y = x + 1$ and $y = x^2 - 4x + 5$
$x+1 = x^2 - 4x + 5 \implies x^2 - 5x + 4 = 0 \implies (x-4)(x-1)=0$. Points: $(4,5), (1,2)$.
3. $y = 2x - 1$ and $y = x^2 - 3x + 2$
$2x - 1 = x^2 - 3x + 2 \implies x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$. Use quadratic formula: $x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{13}}{2}$.
4. $y = -x$ and $y = x^2 + 2x - 3$
$-x = x^2 + 2x - 3 \implies x^2 + 3x - 3 = 0$. $x = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{21}}{2}$.
5. $y = 4$ and $y = x^2 - 4x$
$4 = x^2 - 4x \implies x^2 - 4x - 4 = 0$. $x = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{32}}{2} = 2 \pm 2\sqrt{2}$.

e. Guided Practice (00:25 - 00:40) Praktik Terbimbing

Explanation Pembahasan

Solving exponential simultaneous equations. Given $3^x = 9^{y-1}$ and $x^2 = y^2 + 7$. Menyelesaikan persamaan simultan eksponensial. Diketahui $3^x = 9^{y-1}$ dan $x^2 = y^2 + 7$.

Change base 9 to 3: $9 = 3^2$. So $3^x = (3^2)^{y-1} = 3^{2(y-1)} = 3^{2y-2}$. Ubah basis 9 ke 3: $9 = 3^2$. Maka $3^x = (3^2)^{y-1} = 3^{2(y-1)} = 3^{2y-2}$.

Since bases are equal, $x = 2y - 2$. Karena basis sama, $x = 2y - 2$.

Substitute $x$ into second equation: $(2y-2)^2 = y^2 + 7$. Substitusikan $x$ ke persamaan kedua: $(2y-2)^2 = y^2 + 7$.

Expand: $4y^2 - 8y + 4 = y^2 + 7 \implies 3y^2 - 8y - 3 = 0$. Sederhanakan: $4y^2 - 8y + 4 = y^2 + 7 \implies 3y^2 - 8y - 3 = 0$.

Factorise: $(3y+1)(y-3) = 0$. So $y = -1/3$ or $y = 3$. Faktorkan: $(3y+1)(y-3) = 0$. Jadi $y = -1/3$ atau $y = 3$.

If $y=3, x = 2(3)-2 = 4$. If $y=-1/3, x = 2(-1/3)-2 = -8/3$. Jika $y=3, x = 2(3)-2 = 4$. Jika $y=-1/3, x = 2(-1/3)-2 = -8/3$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. Prove $x = 3y - 3$ if $2^x = 8^{y-1}$
$8 = 2^3$. $2^x = (2^3)^{y-1} = 2^{3(y-1)} = 2^{3y-3}$. Thus $x = 3y-3$.
2. Solve $x = 3y - 3$ and $x^2 = y^2 + 5$
$(3y-3)^2 = y^2 + 5 \implies 9y^2 - 18y + 9 = y^2 + 5 \implies 8y^2 - 18y + 4 = 0 \implies 4y^2 - 9y + 2 = 0$. $(4y-1)(y-2)=0$. $y=1/4$ or $y=2$.
3. Prove $x = 2y - 1$ if $5^x = 25^{y-0.5}$
$25 = 5^2$. $5^x = (5^2)^{y-0.5} = 5^{2(y-0.5)} = 5^{2y-1}$. Thus $x = 2y-1$.
4. Solve $x = 2y - 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 10$
$(2y-1)^2 + y^2 = 10 \implies 4y^2 - 4y + 1 + y^2 = 10 \implies 5y^2 - 4y - 9 = 0$. $(5y-9)(y+1)=0$. $y=9/5$ or $y=-1$.
5. Solve $4^x = 2^{y+2}$ and $x+y=5$
$2^{2x} = 2^{y+2} \implies 2x = y+2 \implies y = 2x-2$. Substitute: $x + (2x-2) = 5 \implies 3x = 7 \implies x = 7/3$. $y = 8/3$.

f. Independent Practice (00:40 - 00:55) Praktik Mandiri

Explanation Pembahasan

Solving $5x + y = 7$ and $3x^2 + y^2 = 21$. Menyelesaikan $5x + y = 7$ dan $3x^2 + y^2 = 21$.

From eq 1: $y = 7 - 5x$. Substitute into eq 2: Dari persamaan 1: $y = 7 - 5x$. Substitusikan ke persamaan 2:

$3x^2 + (7 - 5x)^2 = 21$. Expand: $3x^2 + 25x^2 - 70x + 49 = 21$. $3x^2 + (7 - 5x)^2 = 21$. Sederhanakan: $3x^2 + 25x^2 - 70x + 49 = 21$.

$28x^2 - 70x + 28 = 0$. Divide by 14: $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$. $28x^2 - 70x + 28 = 0$. Bagi dengan 14: $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$.

Factorise: $(2x-1)(x-2) = 0$. $x = 1/2$ or $x = 2$. Faktorkan: $(2x-1)(x-2) = 0$. $x = 1/2$ atau $x = 2$.

If $x=1/2, y = 7 - 5(1/2) = 4.5$. If $x=2, y = 7 - 10 = -3$. Jika $x=1/2, y = 7 - 5(1/2) = 4.5$. Jika $x=2, y = 7 - 10 = -3$.

Task Latihan Soal

1. $y = 3x - 2$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 5$
$x^2 + (3x-2)^2 = 5 \implies x^2 + 9x^2 - 12x + 4 = 5 \implies 10x^2 - 12x - 1 = 0$. Use formula.
2. $y = x + 1$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 13$
$x^2 + (x+1)^2 = 13 \implies 2x^2 + 2x + 1 = 13 \implies 2x^2 + 2x - 12 = 0 \implies x^2 + x - 6 = 0$. Points: $(2,3), (-3,-2)$.
3. $2x + y = 5$ and $x^2 - y^2 = 7$
$y = 5 - 2x$. $x^2 - (5-2x)^2 = 7 \implies x^2 - (25 - 20x + 4x^2) = 7 \implies -3x^2 + 20x - 32 = 0$.
4. $x - y = 2$ and $xy = 15$
$x = y + 2$. $(y+2)y = 15 \implies y^2 + 2y - 15 = 0 \implies (y+5)(y-3)=0$. $y=3, x=5$ or $y=-5, x=-3$.
5. $y = 2x - 3$ and $x^2 - 2y = 1$
$x^2 - 2(2x - 3) = 1 \implies x^2 - 4x + 6 = 1 \implies x^2 - 4x + 5 = 0$. No real roots (discriminant < 0).
