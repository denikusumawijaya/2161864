Topic: Rationalizing Denominators

(Bahasa Inggris) Rationalizing denominators is the process of eliminating radical expressions from the bottom of a fraction.

(Bahasa Indonesia) Merasionalkan penyebut adalah proses menghilangkan bentuk akar dari bagian bawah suatu pecahan.

1. Spiral Review Warm-up (00:00-00:10)

Theory: Visualizing Surds & Place Value

A "surd" is a number that cannot be simplified to remove a square root (or cube root etc.). For example, \(\sqrt{2}\) is a surd, but \(\sqrt{4}\) is not because it equals 2.

Bentuk akar (surds) adalah angka yang tidak dapat disederhanakan untuk menghilangkan akar kuadrat. Misalnya, \(\sqrt{2}\) adalah bentuk akar, tetapi \(\sqrt{4}\) bukan karena sama dengan 2.

To simplify \(\sqrt{72}\), we look for the largest perfect square factor. \(72 = 36 \times 2\). So, \(\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2}\).

Untuk menyederhanakan \(\sqrt{72}\), kita cari faktor kuadrat sempurna terbesar. \(72 = 36 \times 2\). Jadi, \(\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 1.1: Simplify \(\sqrt{50}\)

Answer: \(5\sqrt{2}\).

Faktor kuadrat sempurna terbesar dari 50 adalah 25. \(\sqrt{50} = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 1.2: What is the area of a square with side \(\sqrt{50}\)?

Answer: 50. Area = side \(\times\) side = \(\sqrt{50} \times \sqrt{50} = 50\).

Luas = sisi \(\times\) sisi = \(\sqrt{50} \times \sqrt{50} = 50\).

Exercise 1.3: Place Value - Write 1.25 Billion in full numerical form.

Answer: 1,250,000,000.

1,25 Miliar dalam bentuk angka penuh adalah 1,250,000,000.

Theory: Simplifying Larger Numbers

Simplifying surds helps in standardizing calculations. If a company has a revenue of 4.5 Trillion, we write it as 4,500,000,000,000. The '5' is in the hundred billions place.

Menyederhanakan bentuk akar membantu menstandarisasi perhitungan. Jika perusahaan memiliki pendapatan 4,5 Triliun, kita menulisnya sebagai 4.500.000.000.000. Angka '5' berada di nilai tempat ratusan miliar.

Exercise 1.4: Simplify \(\sqrt{18} + \sqrt{8}\)

Answer: \(5\sqrt{2}\). \(\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}\) and \(\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}\). Sum = \(5\sqrt{2}\).

\(\sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}\) dan \(\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}\). Jumlah = \(5\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 1.5: Place Value - What is the difference between \(10^6\) and \(10^4\)?

Answer: 990,000. \(1,000,000 - 10,000 = 990,000\).

\(1.000.000 - 10.000 = 990.000\).

Exercise 1.6: Simplify \(\sqrt{72}\)

Answer: \(6\sqrt{2}\).

Faktor kuadrat sempurna terbesar dari 72 adalah 36. \(\sqrt{72} = \sqrt{36 \times 2} = 6\sqrt{2}\).

Theory: Identifying Surds in Fractions

Sometimes surds appear in fractions like \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\). While mathematically correct, it's often preferred to have rational numbers (no roots) in the denominator.

Terkadang bentuk akar muncul di pecahan seperti \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\). Meskipun secara matematis benar, lebih disukai untuk memiliki bilangan rasional (tanpa akar) di penyebut.

Exercise 1.7: Which of the following has an irrational denominator?

Answer: \(\frac{3}{\sqrt{5}}\). \(\sqrt{5}\) is not a rational number.

\(\sqrt{5}\) bukan bilangan rasional.

Exercise 1.8: Simplify \(\frac{\sqrt{25}}{5}\)

Answer: 1. \(\sqrt{25} = 5\), so \(\frac{5}{5} = 1\).

\(\sqrt{25} = 5\), jadi \(\frac{5}{5} = 1\).

Exercise 1.9: What is \(\sqrt{100}\)?

Answer: 10. \(10 \times 10 = 100\).

\(10 \times 10 = 100\).

Exercise 1.10: Place Value - In 4,500,000,000,000, what is the value of '5'?

Answer: 500,000,000,000 (500 Billion).

Nilai tempat '5' adalah 500 Miliar.

2. Introduction & Real-World Connection (00:10-00:15)

Theory: Why Rationalize?

In business and math, we prefer "clean" numbers. Imagine splitting a $1 profit among \(\sqrt{2}\) partners. It's hard to visualize \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\). Rationalizing cleans up the fraction so it is professional and easy to work with.

Dalam bisnis dan matematika, kita lebih suka angka yang "bersih". Bayangkan membagi keuntungan $1 di antara \(\sqrt{2}\) mitra. Sulit membayangkan \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\). Merasionalkan membersihkan pecahan agar profesional dan mudah digunakan.

Exercise 2.1: Which fraction looks more "professional" or standard?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{2}\). We prefer rational denominators.

Kita lebih menyukai penyebut rasional.

Exercise 2.2: Why do we rationalize denominators?

Answer: For standardized numerical representation.

Untuk representasi numerik yang standar.

Exercise 2.3: A company splits $5 among \(\sqrt{5}\) partners. What is the fraction representing one partner's share?

Answer: \(\frac{5}{\sqrt{5}}\).

Bagian satu mitra adalah \(\frac{5}{\sqrt{5}}\).

Theory: The "Unpolished" Fraction

Having a radical in the denominator is like an unpolished business proposal. We refine it to make it presentable.

Memiliki akar di penyebut seperti proposal bisnis yang belum dipoles. Kami memolesnya agar layak dipresentasikan.

Exercise 2.4: Rationalizing is like... ?

Answer: Refining raw materials.

Menyempurnakan bahan baku.

Exercise 2.5: Identify the denominator in \(\frac{3}{\sqrt{7}}\).

Answer: \(\sqrt{7}\).

Penyebutnya adalah \(\sqrt{7}\).

Exercise 2.6: Is \(\frac{1}{4}\) rationalized?

Answer: Yes, because 4 is not a surd.

Ya, karena 4 bukan bentuk akar.

Theory: Comparing \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}\)

We prefer \(\frac{1}{2}\) over \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}\) even though they are equal. It's cleaner.

Kita lebih suka \(\frac{1}{2}\) daripada \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}\) meskipun nilainya sama. Itu lebih bersih.

Exercise 2.7: Evaluate \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}\).

Answer: 0.5 (or \(\frac{1}{2}\)).

\(\sqrt{4} = 2\), jadi \(\frac{1}{2} = 0.5\).

Exercise 2.8: Which is the "messy" version?

Answer: \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{4}}\).

Versi yang berantakan adalah yang memiliki akar di penyebut.

Exercise 2.9: Does rationalizing change the value of the fraction?

Answer: No, it only changes the look.

Tidak, itu hanya mengubah tampilannya, bukan nilainya.

Exercise 2.10: A fraction has a denominator of \(\sqrt{9}\). Is it rationalized?

Answer: No, it should be simplified to 3 first.

Tidak, harus disederhanakan menjadi 3 dulu.

3. Review of Prior Knowledge (00:15-00:20)

Theory: The Golden Rule of Fractions

Whatever you do to the bottom, you must do to the top to keep the value the same. If you multiply the denominator by \(\sqrt{2}\), you must multiply the numerator by \(\sqrt{2}\).

Apa pun yang Anda lakukan pada bagian bawah, Anda harus melakukannya pada bagian atas agar nilainya tetap sama. Jika Anda mengalikan penyebut dengan \(\sqrt{2}\), Anda harus mengalikan pembilang dengan \(\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 3.1: If you multiply the denominator by 3, what must you do to the numerator?

Answer: Multiply by 3.

Kalikan dengan 3.

Exercise 3.2: What is \(\frac{2}{2}\)?

Answer: 1.

2 dibagi 2 adalah 1.

Exercise 3.3: What is \(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5}}\)?

Answer: 1.

Akar 5 dibagi akar 5 adalah 1.

Theory: Multiplying Surds

What happens when you multiply \(\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3}\)? It becomes 3. \( \sqrt{x} \times \sqrt{x} = x \). This is our secret weapon for rationalizing.

Apa yang terjadi ketika Anda mengalikan \(\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3}\)? Itu menjadi 3. \( \sqrt{x} \times \sqrt{x} = x \). Ini adalah senjata rahasia kita untuk merasionalkan.

Exercise 3.4: Calculate \(\sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{7}\).

Answer: 7.

Akar 7 dikali akar 7 adalah 7.

Exercise 3.5: Simplify \(\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8}\).

Answer: 4. (\(\sqrt{16} = 4\)).

Akar 2 kali akar 8 adalah akar 16, yang sama dengan 4.

Exercise 3.6: Calculate \(\sqrt{10} \times \sqrt{10}\).

Answer: 10.

Akar 10 dikali akar 10 adalah 10.

Theory: Algebraic Identity

The difference of squares: \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\). We use this to get rid of binomial surds (two terms with roots).

Selisih kuadrat: \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\). Kami menggunakan ini untuk menghilangkan bentuk akar binomial (dua suku dengan akar).

Exercise 3.7: Expand \((x+2)(x-2)\).

Answer: \(x^2 - 4\).

Pakai rumus \((a+b)(a-b) = a^2 - b^2\). Jadi \(x^2 - 2^2 = x^2 - 4\).

Exercise 3.8: Expand \((\sqrt{3}+1)(\sqrt{3}-1)\).

Answer: 2. (\((\sqrt{3})^2 - 1^2 = 3 - 1 = 2\)).

Akar 3 kuadrat adalah 3. Jadi \(3 - 1 = 2\).

Exercise 3.9: Expand \((5+\sqrt{2})(5-\sqrt{2})\).

Answer: 23. (\(25 - 2 = 23\)).

\(5^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 25 - 2 = 23\).

Exercise 3.10: What is the conjugate of \(3 - \sqrt{5}\)?

Answer: \(3 + \sqrt{5}\). Just change the middle sign.

Ubah tanda di tengah. Jadi dari minus jadi plus.

4. Problem Introduction (00:20-00:30)

Theory: The "Special Version of 1"

Look at \(\frac{5}{\sqrt{3}}\). To get rid of the root at the bottom, we multiply by a "special version of 1". Since \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = 1\), we aren't changing the value, just the look.

Lihatlah \(\frac{5}{\sqrt{3}}\). Untuk menghilangkan akar di bawah, kita kalikan dengan "versi khusus dari 1". Karena \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = 1\), kita tidak mengubah nilai, hanya tampilannya.

Exercise 4.1: What is the "special version of 1" used to rationalize \(\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\)?

Answer: \(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5}}\). We always use the same root as the denominator.

Kita selalu menggunakan akar yang sama dengan penyebutnya.

Exercise 4.2: Why does multiplying by \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}\) not change the value of the fraction?

Answer: Because \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}\) equals 1, and multiplying by 1 doesn't change the value.

Karena \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}}\) sama dengan 1, dan mengalikan dengan 1 tidak mengubah nilai.

Exercise 4.3: What is the first step to rationalize \(\frac{7}{\sqrt{10}}\)?

Answer: Multiply both top and bottom by \(\sqrt{10}\).

Kalikan atas dan bawah dengan \(\sqrt{10}\).

Theory: The Conjugate Method

For harder problems like \(\frac{2}{3+\sqrt{2}}\), multiplying by \(\frac{3+\sqrt{2}}{3+\sqrt{2}}\) won't help (it makes it worse!). We use the "Conjugate"—the same numbers but with the opposite sign in the middle: \(3-\sqrt{2}\).

Untuk soal yang lebih sulit seperti \(\frac{2}{3+\sqrt{2}}\), mengalikan dengan \(\frac{3+\sqrt{2}}{3+\sqrt{2}}\) tidak membantu. Kita gunakan "Konjugat"—angka yang sama tetapi dengan tanda berlawanan di tengah: \(3-\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 4.4: What is the conjugate of \(4+\sqrt{5}\)?

Answer: \(4-\sqrt{5}\). Just change the plus to a minus.

Ubah tanda plus menjadi minus.

Exercise 4.5: What is the conjugate of \(\sqrt{7}-2\)?

Answer: \(\sqrt{7}+2\). Change the minus to a plus.

Ubah tanda minus menjadi plus.

Exercise 4.6: To rationalize \(\frac{5}{2-\sqrt{3}}\), what should you multiply top and bottom by?

Answer: \(2+\sqrt{3}\).

Kalikan dengan konjugatnya, yaitu \(2+\sqrt{3}\).

Theory: The Disappearing Middle

Watch how the middle terms disappear when we use the conjugate! \((a-b)(a+b) = a^2 - b^2\). Example: \((3-\sqrt{2})(3+\sqrt{2}) = 3^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 9 - 2 = 7\). The radical is gone!

Perhatikan bagaimana suku tengah hilang saat kita menggunakan konjugat! \((a-b)(a+b) = a^2 - b^2\). Contoh: \((3-\sqrt{2})(3+\sqrt{2}) = 3^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 9 - 2 = 7\). Bentuk akarnya hilang!

Exercise 4.7: Expand \((2-\sqrt{3})(2+\sqrt{3})\).

Answer: 1. \(2^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2 = 4 - 3 = 1\).

\(2^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2 = 4 - 3 = 1\).

Exercise 4.8: Expand \((\sqrt{5}-1)(\sqrt{5}+1)\).

Answer: 4. \((\sqrt{5})^2 - 1^2 = 5 - 1 = 4\).

\((\sqrt{5})^2 - 1^2 = 5 - 1 = 4\).

Exercise 4.9: What is the denominator result of \(\frac{1}{5+\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{5-\sqrt{2}}{5-\sqrt{2}}\)?

Answer: 23. \(5^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 25 - 2 = 23\).

\(5^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 25 - 2 = 23\).

Exercise 4.10: What is the denominator result of \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{7}-3}\) after multiplying by its conjugate?

Answer: -2. \((\sqrt{7})^2 - 3^2 = 7 - 9 = -2\). It is perfectly fine to have a negative rational number in the denominator.

\((\sqrt{7})^2 - 3^2 = 7 - 9 = -2\). Tidak masalah jika hasilnya bilangan rasional negatif.

5. Guided Practice (00:30-00:45)

Theory: Building a "Financial Model" (Monomial)

Let's rationalize \(\frac{6}{\sqrt{2}}\). Multiply top and bottom by \(\sqrt{2}\). Bottom becomes 2. Top becomes \(6\sqrt{2}\). Result: \(\frac{6\sqrt{2}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}\). Always simplify your final answer!

Mari kita rasionalkan \(\frac{6}{\sqrt{2}}\). Kalikan atas dan bawah dengan \(\sqrt{2}\). Bawah menjadi 2. Atas menjadi \(6\sqrt{2}\). Hasil: \(\frac{6\sqrt{2}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}\). Selalu sederhanakan jawaban akhir Anda!

Exercise 5.1: Rationalize and simplify \(\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}}\).

Answer: \(\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}\). It cannot be simplified further.

Hasilnya \(\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}\). Tidak bisa disederhanakan lagi.

Exercise 5.2: Rationalize and simplify \(\frac{10}{\sqrt{5}}\).

Answer: \(2\sqrt{5}\). \(\frac{10\sqrt{5}}{5} = 2\sqrt{5}\).

\(\frac{10\sqrt{5}}{5} = 2\sqrt{5}\).

Exercise 5.3: Rationalize and simplify \(\frac{\sqrt{7}}{\sqrt{2}}\).

Answer: \(\frac{\sqrt{14}}{2}\). \(\sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{2} = \sqrt{14}\).

\(\sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{2} = \sqrt{14}\).

Theory: Rationalizing Binomial Denominators

Let's try \(\frac{4}{\sqrt{5}-1}\). Multiply by \(\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{\sqrt{5}+1}\). Denominator: \((\sqrt{5})^2 - 1^2 = 5-1=4\). Numerator: \(4(\sqrt{5}+1)\). Result: \(\frac{4(\sqrt{5}+1)}{4} = \sqrt{5}+1\). The middle terms disappear!

Mari kita coba \(\frac{4}{\sqrt{5}-1}\). Kalikan dengan \(\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{\sqrt{5}+1}\). Penyebut: \((\sqrt{5})^2 - 1^2 = 5-1=4\). Pembilang: \(4(\sqrt{5}+1)\). Hasil: \(\frac{4(\sqrt{5}+1)}{4} = \sqrt{5}+1\). Suku tengahnya hilang!

Exercise 5.4: What is the numerator of \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+3}\) after multiplying by its conjugate?

Answer: \(\sqrt{2}-3\). We multiply the top by the conjugate \(\sqrt{2}-3\).

Kita kalikan atas dengan konjugatnya \(\sqrt{2}-3\).

Exercise 5.5: What is the denominator of \(\frac{5}{2-\sqrt{3}}\) after multiplying by its conjugate?

Answer: 1. \(2^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2 = 4 - 3 = 1\).

\(2^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2 = 4 - 3 = 1\).

Exercise 5.6: Simplify \(\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}-1}\).

Answer: \(2(\sqrt{2}+1)\) or \(2\sqrt{2}+2\). Denominator becomes 1.

Penyebut menjadi 1. Hasilnya \(2(\sqrt{2}+1)\) atau \(2\sqrt{2}+2\).

Theory: Accuracy over Speed

In high-stakes business, accuracy is more important than speed. When expanding the numerator, be careful with the signs! \(4 \times (\sqrt{5}+1) = 4\sqrt{5} + 4\). Don't accidentally multiply the 4 into the square root! \(4\sqrt{5}\) is NOT \(\sqrt{20}\).

Dalam bisnis berisiko tinggi, akurasi lebih penting daripada kecepatan. Saat menguraikan pembilang, berhati-hatilah dengan tanda! \(4 \times (\sqrt{5}+1) = 4\sqrt{5} + 4\). Jangan kalikan 4 ke dalam akar! \(4\sqrt{5}\) BUKAN \(\sqrt{20}\).

Exercise 5.7: Expand \(6(\sqrt{3}-2)\).

Answer: \(6\sqrt{3}-12\). Distribute the 6, don't multiply it into the root.

Bagikan 6 tersebut, jangan dikalikan ke dalam akar.

Exercise 5.8: Simplify \(\frac{10(\sqrt{3}-1)}{2}\).

Answer: \(5(\sqrt{3}-1)\) or \(5\sqrt{3}-5\). Divide 10 by 2.

Bagi 10 dengan 2. Hasilnya \(5(\sqrt{3}-1)\) atau \(5\sqrt{3}-5\).

Exercise 5.9: Rationalize \(\frac{3}{\sqrt{7}-\sqrt{2}}\). What is the denominator?

Answer: 5. \((\sqrt{7})^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 = 7 - 2 = 5\).

\(7 - 2 = 5\).

Exercise 5.10: Which of these is equivalent to \(\sqrt{20}\)?

Answer: \(2\sqrt{5}\). \(\sqrt{4 \times 5} = \sqrt{4} \times \sqrt{5} = 2\sqrt{5}\). It is NOT \(4\sqrt{5}\).

\(\sqrt{4 \times 5} = \sqrt{4} \times \sqrt{5} = 2\sqrt{5}\). Bukan \(4\sqrt{5}\).

6. Independent Practice (00:50-01:00)

Theory: Client Cases - Double Roots

Sometimes you have roots on both top and bottom, like \(\frac{10}{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}\). The conjugate is \(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}\). Denominator becomes \(6-2=4\). Numerator: \(10(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2})\).

Terkadang Anda memiliki akar di atas dan bawah, seperti \(\frac{10}{\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2}}\). Konjugatnya adalah \(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}\). Penyebut menjadi \(6-2=4\). Pembilang: \(10(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2})\).

Exercise 6.1: What is the conjugate of \(\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}\)?

Answer: \(\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}\).

Ubah tanda di tengah.

Exercise 6.2: Multiply \((\sqrt{6}+\sqrt{2})(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2})\).

Answer: 4. \(6 - 2 = 4\).

\(6 - 2 = 4\).

Exercise 6.3: Simplify \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}-\sqrt{3}}\).

Answer: \(\frac{\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{3}}{2}\). Denominator is \(5-3=2\).

Penyebutnya \(5-3=2\).

Theory: Complex Expressions

What if the numerator is not a simple number? Like \(\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{\sqrt{3}-1}\). We still multiply top and bottom by \(\sqrt{3}+1\). Denominator: 2. Numerator: \((\sqrt{3}+1)^2 = 3 + 2\sqrt{3} + 1 = 4 + 2\sqrt{3}\).

Bagaimana jika pembilangnya bukan angka sederhana? Seperti \(\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{\sqrt{3}-1}\). Kita tetap kalikan atas dan bawah dengan \(\sqrt{3}+1\). Penyebut: 2. Pembilang: \((\sqrt{3}+1)^2 = 3 + 2\sqrt{3} + 1 = 4 + 2\sqrt{3}\).

Exercise 6.4: Expand \((\sqrt{3}+1)^2\).

Answer: \(4 + 2\sqrt{3}\). Use \((a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2\).

Gunakan rumus \((a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2\). \(3 + 2\sqrt{3} + 1\).

Exercise 6.5: Expand \((\sqrt{2}-3)^2\).

Answer: \(11 - 6\sqrt{2}\). \(2 - 6\sqrt{2} + 9\).

\(2 - 6\sqrt{2} + 9 = 11 - 6\sqrt{2}\).

Exercise 6.6: Rationalize \(\frac{\sqrt{2}+2}{\sqrt{2}-1}\). What is the denominator?

Answer: 1. \(2 - 1 = 1\).

\(2 - 1 = 1\).

Theory: Why It Matters (Calculus Prep)

You might wonder why we do this. In Calculus, if you have a limit where x approaches a number that makes the denominator zero, rationalizing can help cancel out the zero and solve the problem. It's a foundational skill for advanced math!

Anda mungkin bertanya mengapa kita melakukan ini. Di Kalkulus, jika Anda memiliki limit di mana x mendekati angka yang membuat penyebut nol, merasionalkan dapat membantu menghilangkan nol dan menyelesaikan masalah. Ini adalah keterampilan dasar untuk matematika tingkat lanjut!

Exercise 6.7: Rationalize \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{8}}\). What is the denominator?

Answer: 2. \(10 - 8 = 2\).

\(10 - 8 = 2\).

Exercise 6.8: Rationalize \(\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{5}+3}\). What is the denominator?

Answer: -4. \(5 - 9 = -4\).

\(5 - 9 = -4\).

Exercise 6.9: Simplify \(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{8}}\). (Hint: \(\sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}\))

Answer: \(\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}\). Rationalize both: \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\). Then find common denominator: \(\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{4} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}\).

Rasionalkan keduanya terlebih dahulu: \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}\).

Exercise 6.10: Challenge: To rationalize \(\frac{1}{1+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}\), what can you group together as 'a' and 'b' in the form \(\frac{1}{a+b}\)?

Answer: Let \(a = 1+\sqrt{2}\) and \(b = \sqrt{3}\). Then the conjugate is \((1+\sqrt{2})-\sqrt{3}\).

Misalkan \(a = 1+\sqrt{2}\) dan \(b = \sqrt{3}\). Maka konjugatnya adalah \((1+\sqrt{2})-\sqrt{3}\).

7. Consolidation & Reflection (01:00-01:05)

Summary: The Art of Cleaning Up Fractions

Fantastic work today! You've mastered the art of cleaning up complex fractions. Remember, rationalizing is just a fancy way of multiplying by 1 to make things look better. We use \(\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x}}\) for simple roots, and the conjugate \((a+b)\) or \((a-b)\) for binomials.

Kerja yang luar biasa hari ini! Anda telah menguasai seni membersihkan pecahan kompleks. Ingat, merasionalkan hanyalah cara mewah mengalikan dengan 1 agar terlihat lebih baik. Kita gunakan \(\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{x}}\) untuk akar sederhana, dan konjugat \((a+b)\) atau \((a-b)\) untuk bentuk binomial.

Which method do we use for \(\frac{3}{\sqrt{5}-2}\)?

Answer: The Conjugate Method. Multiply by \(\frac{\sqrt{5}+2}{\sqrt{5}+2}\).

Metode Konjugat. Kalikan dengan \(\frac{\sqrt{5}+2}{\sqrt{5}+2}\).

Does rationalizing change the actual value of the number?

Answer: No, it only changes the form (look) of the number.

Tidak, itu hanya mengubah bentuk (tampilan) angka tersebut.
